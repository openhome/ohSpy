## 2026-05-31

- **FR-102 added: `<allowedValueList>` surfaced as a constrained selector in the action invocation popup.** Simon's call on draft review (Open Question §10.3 resolved partially). Scope: only `<allowedValueList>` (string enumerations) — `<allowedValueRange>` (numeric min/max/step) remains deferred. Why: enumerated values are the common case (transport state, source selection, room mode); dropdown UX prevents trivial typo-fault round-trips. How to apply: PRD now treats `<allowedValueList>` handling as a v1 capability; architecture phase needs to confirm UI control choice (combo vs radio) and the empty/malformed-list fallback path. Non-Goal narrowed to "`<allowedValueRange>` validation"; Open Question §10.3 reframed to track the remaining range-validation gap.
- **FR-103 added: `<allowedValueRange>` surfaced as a bounded numeric input in the action invocation popup.** Simon's call same-day, pulling the remaining half of the original Open Question §10.3 into scope. Scope: numeric `<minimum>`/`<maximum>`/`<step>` from SCPD enforced client-side; default-value pre-populate if it satisfies range+step, else `<minimum>`; malformed-range fallback to free-form with Warning diagnostic; mutual exclusion with FR-102 resolved in FR-102's favour. Why: range constraints are common on volume, gain, balance, source-index etc. and a client-side bound prevents trivial out-of-range faults. How to apply: architecture phase needs to confirm the numeric control (WinUI 3 `NumberBox` natively supports min/max/step) and define behaviour at step boundaries. Non-Goal reframed to "no `<dataType>`-driven typed inputs"; Open Question §10.3 now tracks only the broader gap of typed inputs beyond list/range (boolean, dateTime, uri, base64).
- **FR-102 and FR-103 framed as deliberate parity-plus additions.** Reviewer Gate scope-discipline review flagged them as outside the brief's "parity + named fixes" envelope. Simon confirmed both stay (2026-05-31). They are documented as **required v1 capability** with explicit "parity-plus" annotation at the FR header and in §0 Document Purpose. Why: enumerated and numeric range constraints prevent trivial round-trip faults during action invocation — a real productivity win for a developer tool, worth the small scope expansion. How to apply: any future re-baseline against UpnpSpy should preserve FR-102 / FR-103 as ohSpy capabilities; downstream architecture, stories, and L&L narrative should treat them as in-scope v1, not as stretch goals or stretch fixes.
