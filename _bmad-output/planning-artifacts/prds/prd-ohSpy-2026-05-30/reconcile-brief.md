# Reconciliation — brief.md vs PRD

## Summary

The PRD carries the brief's functional substance faithfully — every named scope item, quality bar, performance constraint, and non-goal from the brief is traceable into a PRD section. However, several **qualitative and meta-framing** elements of the brief have been softened or silently dropped: the brief's distinctive voice ("terse, dense, fast", "no tutorials, no hand-holding") survives in only one sentence of §1; the **"If It Doesn't Land" fallback** is entirely absent; the **"Process Artifacts as First-Class Deliverables"** framing is mentioned but not given equivalent weight; and the explicit risk-honesty ("The brief presumes success. It does not depend on it.") is gone. The dual-purpose framing (tool + advocacy demo) is preserved, but in a more confident register than the brief's modest one.

## Gaps (severity-ranked)

- **high** "If It Doesn't Land" fallback section silently dropped (brief §"If It Doesn't Land" → no PRD equivalent) — The brief explicitly frames two failure modes (BMad result no better than UpnpSpy → no talk; talk happens and lands flat → no harm done) and concludes "The brief presumes success. It does not depend on it." None of this risk-honesty appears in the PRD. The PRD's Success Metrics (SM-5, SM-6) assume the L&L happens and lands. Downstream readers (architect, story writer) will not encounter the brief's explicit permission to ship the tool even if the talk doesn't materialise. *Fix:* Add a short subsection to §9 Success Metrics (or a new §12 "Risk Framing") that carries the brief's fallback verbatim or paraphrased — particularly the "tool persists, artifacts persist" point and the explicit decoupling of tool-success from talk-success.

- **high** "Process Artifacts as First-Class Deliverables" framing weakened (brief §"Process Artifacts as First-Class Deliverables" → PRD §0 / §1 / SM-6) — The brief's framing is bold: "The build is the talk; the artifacts are the script." and "Quality and clarity in these documents matters as much as quality in the binary." The PRD acknowledges artifacts as deliverables (§0 closing paragraph, §1 closing paragraph, SM-5/SM-6) but never asserts equivalence between artifact quality and binary quality. This matters because the PRD is itself one of those artifacts — and downstream agents need to know the quality bar applies to *them*, not just to the running app. *Fix:* Add an explicit statement (in §0 or as a sibling to §5 Cross-Cutting NFRs) that artifact quality is co-equal with product quality, lifted from the brief's wording.

- **medium** Brief's qualitative voice / tone of audience flattened (brief §"Who This Serves", §"What Makes This Different") — The brief uses pungent characterisations: users are "UPnP-literate, technical, impatient with bad tools", they want "dense readable display of device internals", "No tutorials, no hand-holding". And: "Honest answer — there is no technical moat." The PRD's §1 Vision captures "terse, dense, fast, no tutorials, no hand-holding" in a single clause, but §2 Target User reframes these users in neutral JTBD prose, losing the impatience-with-bad-tools edge entirely. The "no technical moat" honesty is gone. *Fix:* Either restore a short "Voice / Tone of this PRD" note in §0, or add a one-line "no technical moat" honesty statement in §1 Vision (e.g. after the existing "It exists because…" paragraph). The voice loss in §2 is harder to fix without rewriting; flag for the architect and story-writer to preserve in their downstream wording.

- **medium** "Owned by a team that uses it daily" — partially carried, framing reduced (brief §"What Makes This Different" → PRD §1 Vision) — The brief positions this as ohSpy's *only* differentiator versus Intel Device Spy ("actively maintained, runs on modern Windows, owned by a team that uses it daily"). The PRD §1 carries "actively maintained, owned by a team that uses it daily" but does not frame it as the *only* differentiator. Downstream readers may interpret feature additions as differentiators in their own right. *Fix:* Reassert in §7 Non-Goals or §0 that there is no other differentiator — this is parity-plus-named-fixes, not parity-plus-anything-clever.

- **medium** Brief's modest Vision framing slightly inflated (brief §"Vision" → PRD §1 Vision) — Brief: "Modest and grounded. … ohSpy is not a product with a roadmap. It is a tool that should keep working." PRD §1: a confident three-paragraph product-vision-style write-up that reads like a roadmap-bearing product. The brief's explicit anti-roadmap clause is gone; the brief's "if Linn wants a public footprint in UPnP dev tools" hedge ("becomes a credible option") is gone; the brief's "L&L outcome may matter more than the tool itself" candor is gone. *Fix:* Append a short closing paragraph to §1 Vision that restores the brief's wording — particularly "not a product with a roadmap" and the L&L-may-matter-more hedge — to anchor downstream agents in the modest register.

- **medium** Two named UpnpSpy performance fixes — wording weakened from "demonstrably absent" (brief §"Success Criteria") → SM-2 carries "demonstrably absent" but the brief's binding phrase "verified by eye in a chatty SSDP environment" is partial — Brief says verification is "by eye in a chatty SSDP environment"; PRD SM-2 carries that wording but loses the brief's framing that this is a *binding* success criterion (in the brief, it sits inside "Product success" bullets that the brief's Schedule clause defers to). *Fix:* In §6 or §9, restate that schedule yields to these criteria (brief's "Schedule yields to these three. The lunch & learn happens when the bars are met, not on a fixed date.").

- **medium** "Schedule yields to these three" — the brief's binding commitment is missing (brief §"Quality constraints" closing → no PRD equivalent) — Brief: "Schedule yields to these three. The lunch & learn happens when the bars are met, not on a fixed date." This is a hard scheduling commitment that frames the entire dev loop. The PRD's §5 Cross-Cutting NFRs say the bars "are not tradeable for schedule" — which is the same idea but stated less viscerally and without the explicit "the L&L moves" consequence. Downstream sprint-plan / story-prioritisation work needs to know this. *Fix:* Add an explicit clause in §5 (preamble) or §9 Success Metrics: "If the three quality bars are not met, the L&L is rescheduled — not the bars relaxed."

- **low** Brief's "Methodology curiosity, unmet" framing of the secondary audience (brief §"The Problem") is dropped (PRD §2.1 JTBD-6 mentions the demonstration; §2.2 lists non-Linn engineers as non-users) — The brief frames the secondary problem as *unmet curiosity* in Linn engineers — "lack a concrete internal example — one built in front of them by a peer using a structured methodology." The PRD's JTBD-6 frames the activity in the operator's voice ("Demonstrate, for Linn peers, what spec-driven AI development looks like end-to-end") but loses the *unmet curiosity* framing of why the demo matters. *Fix:* Optional — add one line to §2.1 or §1 explaining that the secondary audience has *unmet* curiosity about AI-assisted methodology and that ohSpy is a credibility play.

- **low** "Built end-to-end through BMad's workflow … and walked through at a Linn engineering lunch & learn" (brief §Executive Summary) — PRD §0 / §1 carry it, but the brief's explicit "walked through" verb (live, in front of peers) is softened to "deliberate, walk-through demonstration" in PRD §1. The live-walkthrough character is mostly preserved. *Fix:* No action required; cosmetic.

- **low** Brief's "A prior Claude + spec-kit implementation of the same problem (`C:\work\UpnpSpy`) informs the FR set" (brief §Executive Summary) is carried but the *spec-kit* origin is dropped — PRD §0 says "lifted from `UpnpSpy/specs/001-upnp-spy-discovery/spec.md`" and "Claude + spec-kit" in §0, so the methodology lineage is preserved. *Fix:* None — actually carried.

- **low** "BMM lane, not WDS" framing (brief §Scope "Out") — carried in PRD §7 Non-Goals — Carried verbatim. Just noting it survived. *Fix:* None.

## Carried + reframed

- **Brief §"The Solution"** (four-bullet capability list) → **PRD §4** (thirteen subsections with FRs): the four capabilities (discover / browse / invoke / subscribe) are expanded into structured FR groups. Reframe is faithful; nothing dropped, much added in operational detail.
- **Brief §"Success Criteria" → Product success** → **PRD §9 SM-1, SM-2, SM-3**: carried with detail (specific FR / NFR references) and counter-metrics added.
- **Brief §"Success Criteria" → Eval / advocacy success** → **PRD §9 SM-5, SM-6**: carried but split into two metrics; the brief's single bar ("attendees can follow the narrative arc … leave curious enough to try BMad on their own work") is preserved verbatim in SM-5.
- **Brief §"Scope" → "Quality constraints (binding, non-negotiable)"** → **PRD §5 Cross-Cutting NFRs**: expanded from three bullets to a structured NFR set (R1–R5, P1–P6, UI1–UI4). Faithful expansion; binding-ness is asserted via "not tradeable for schedule" but loses the visceral "schedule yields" wording (see Gaps).
- **Brief §"Scope" → "Devices in scope"** → **PRD §9 SM-1 + §5.1 NFR-R1**: carried; the "adversarial / fuzz-style malformed UPnP" exclusion makes it explicitly into the PRD non-goals and the NFR-R1 out-of-scope note.
- **Brief §"What Makes This Different"** → **PRD §1 Vision paragraph 2** (Wireshark + hand-typed SOAP envelopes fallback): carried in a single sentence. The "ad-hoc alternatives" comparison from the brief is captured.
- **Brief §"Who This Serves"** → **PRD §2.1 JTBD list + §2.2 Non-Users**: reframed from brief's audience-description prose into JTBD bullets + non-user list. Functionally complete; voice softened (see Gaps).

## Out-of-scope by design

- **Brief §"Executive Summary" + §"The Solution"** mention of BMad workflow phases (brief → PRD → architecture → stories → sprint plan) → carried in PRD §0 / §8.1 as workflow context; deeper specifics belong in the architecture document and the BMad meta-spec. Correctly *not* expanded in this PRD.
- **Brief addendum reference** (technical stack — WinUI 3, raw sockets, etc.) → correctly deferred to the architecture phase per PRD §0 and §11 Assumptions Index. Properly out of scope.
- **Brief §Scope → "Distribution — unsigned installer"** → carried in PRD §8.1 as "unsigned MSIX installer". The MSIX specificity is an architecture-adjacent detail; acceptable to surface here.
- **Brief §Scope → "Diagnostics — rolling log file plus in-memory buffer"** → carried into PRD §4.12 as full FR group (FR-039–FR-042). Detail addition is correct PRD-layer work.
