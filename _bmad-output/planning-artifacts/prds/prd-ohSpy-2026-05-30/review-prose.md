## Patterns Observed (not individually fixed)

- **"successfully" / "successful" as a hedge.** Appears in §3 (Glossary, DescriptionFetchState — "fetched and parsed successfully"), §4.1, §4.2. Each instance is technically accurate but the surrounding state machine already implies success. Most can be cut without loss. Only one is listed above (§4.1) — the rest are inside Glossary definitions which the brief excludes from editing.
- **"MUST NOT" stack rhythm.** Consistent across §4 — preserved (it is the doc's contract voice).
- **Em-dash usage.** Dense but consistent; mostly working. Two instances flagged where they stack into hard-to-parse parentheticals (§5 lead, §5.1 NFR-R3).
- **Voice.** Stable terse-technical throughout. No section talks down to a UPnP-fluent reader; the Glossary explicitly disclaims its existence for the L&L audience.
